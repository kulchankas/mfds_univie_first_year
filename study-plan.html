Universität Wien · exam season

The 6-week plan

One-and-a-half months, three exams — Algorithms & Data Structures, Analysis, Linear Algebra — run as parallel tracks on this site's materials. Two content passes, then proofs from memory, then timed mocks, then a taper.

The weekly rhythm

Rotate one primary subject per day (~2.5–3.5 h deep work), six days a week, plus a daily 20–30 min flashcard block that is never skipped — spaced repetition only works if the reviews happen on schedule.

Ground rules
  • Active > passive. "Read the proof" always means: read it once, close the page, reproduce it on paper, compare.
  • Match the exam format. AlgoDat is a written theory exam (the real first-date paper shows the format exactly); prepare by writing, not recognizing.
  • If a week slips, cut breadth, not the mock weeks — weeks 5–6 are the highest-value part of the plan.
  • Linear algebra track below follows the current course (Mula & Wu, LA for Data Science). If your LA exam is the intro course instead, swap the readings for the exam notes and use the five mock exams in weeks 5–6.

Week by week

Week 1 — Foundations, pass I

re-open every subject; find your gaps early
Algorithms

Read the data-structure notes end to end (heaps → BST/RBT → hashing). Do drill groups A–B. Skim the real exam paper without solutions — just to calibrate what's coming.

Analysis

Re-read study guide Blocks 1–2 (metric spaces, completeness/compactness, continuity). Start daily-practice sheets 1–3.

Linear Algebra

Chapters 1–2 of the self-study plan (vector spaces, inner products): watch the 3B1B clips, redo the flagged course exercises for Ch 1.

Milestone: both flashcard decks started; a written list of your 5 weakest topics per subject.

Week 2 — Foundations, pass II

finish the content sweep
Algorithms

Read the proofs page ①–③ (loop invariants, Master machinery, Akra–Bazzi) actively. Finish drill groups C–D; redo any miss the next day.

Analysis

Blocks 3–6 of the study guide (incl. convexity). Daily-practice sheets 4–7. Check the exam-focus table and mark every ✅ topic you can't yet prove.

Linear Algebra

Chapters 3–4 (bilinear forms, determinants): course exercises for Ch 2–3, plus the Ch 4 determinant drills from the weekly-homework table.

Milestone: every drill in the recurrence bank solved at least once; Analysis ✅-list complete.

Week 3 — Proofs from memory, I

closed book: write, compare, repeat
Algorithms

From memory, on paper: Huffman optimality (the 8-pointer — ⑩), bubblesort and insertion-sort invariant proofs (①). Compare against the model solutions; repeat failures two days later.

Analysis

The ✅ exam-priority proofs from memory, one block per session (Banach FPT and friends). Daily-practice sheets 8–10.

Linear Algebra

Chapter 5 (eigenvalues): diagonalization workflow + Cayley–Hamilton; do the flagged Ch 5 course exercises (they were the bulk of the homework schedule).

Milestone: Huffman proof reproduced twice without peeking; eigen-workflow (char. poly → eigenspaces → diagonalizable?) automatic.

Week 4 — Proofs from memory, II

the rotation candidates
Algorithms

From memory: quicksort Thm 7.4 (⑤), lower bound Thm 8.1 (⑥), selection Thm 9.2 (⑦), hashing Thms 11.2/11.8 (⑧). Rehearse writing MIN-HEAPIFY / DECREASE-KEY / RB-INSERT-FIXUP pseudocode cold.

Analysis

⚠️ proofs (the "maybe" tier) + redo the highest-priority problem-set questions (✅ tags on the Analysis page). Daily-practice sheets 11–13.

Linear Algebra

Chapter 6 (spectral theorems, SVD — new in the textbook): compute one full SVD by hand on a $2\times2$, verify with numpy.linalg.svd; Gram–Schmidt and orthogonal-projection drills.

Milestone: all four AlgoDat "big proofs" written once from memory; SVD by hand once without notes.

Week 5 — Mock week

timed, closed book, graded honestly
Algorithms

Sit the real first-date paper under exam conditions; grade with the grader notes; write down every dropped point. Two days later: the predicted second-date paper, same protocol.

Analysis

Self-test: pick one ✅ problem from each problem sheet (2, 3, 5, 8, 11, 14) and solve under time; redo the study-guide recipes for anything that failed.

Linear Algebra

Simulated exam from the course exercises: one exercise per chapter, 90 minutes, closed book. (Intro-LA takers: this is the week for mock exams 1–3.)

Milestone: a one-page "dropped points" list per subject — this is your week-6 curriculum.

Week 6 — Close the gaps, then taper

only what the mocks exposed
Algorithms

Re-do exactly the failed sub-questions from week 5, from scratch. Re-write the two weakest proofs from memory one final time. Skim the notes tables (runtimes, RB properties, Master cases) the day before.

Analysis

Dropped-points list only; then one final pass over the exam-focus table. No new material after mid-week.

Linear Algebra

Dropped-points list; final pass over definitions (norms, inner products, diagonalizability criteria, SVD statement). (Intro-LA: mocks 4–5 early in the week.)

Milestone: last 2 days = flashcards + skim only. Sleep. An exam is a performance, not a cram.

If you have less than 6 weeks left

  • 4 weeks: merge weeks 1–2 into one (skip what you already know cold), keep weeks 3–6 intact.
  • 2 weeks: per subject, do only: the mock (AlgoDat: real paper), the top-priority proofs (AlgoDat: Huffman + loop invariants + recurrences; Analysis: the ✅ list; LA: eigen/SVD workflow), and daily flashcards.
