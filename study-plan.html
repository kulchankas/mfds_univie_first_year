Universität Wien · exam season

The 6-week plan

Three exams — Algorithms & Data Structures, Analysis, Linear Algebra — run as parallel tracks on this site's materials. Two content passes, then proofs from memory, then timed mocks, then a taper.

The weekly rhythm

Rotate one primary subject per day (~2.5–3.5 h deep work), six days a week, plus a daily 20–30 min flashcard block that is never skipped — spaced repetition only works if the reviews happen on schedule.

Ground rules
  • Active > passive. "Read the proof" always means: read it once, close the page, reproduce it on paper, compare.
  • Match the exam format. AlgoDat is a written theory exam (the real first-date paper shows the format exactly); prepare by writing, not recognising.
  • If a week slips, cut breadth, not the mock weeks — weeks 5–6 are the highest-value part of the plan.
  • Linear algebra below follows the current course (Mula & Wu, LA for Data Science). If your LA exam is the intro course instead, swap in the exam notes and the five mock exams.

Week by week

If you have less than 6 weeks left

  • 4 weeks: merge weeks 1–2 into one (skip what you already know cold), keep weeks 3–6 intact.
  • 2 weeks: per subject, do only: the mock, the top-priority proofs (AlgoDat: Huffman + loop invariants + recurrences; Analysis: the ✅ list; LA: SVD + spectral + eigen workflow), and daily flashcards.
